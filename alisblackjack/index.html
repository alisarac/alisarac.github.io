Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Alis Blackjack ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: Alis Blackjack is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. All your progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information We DO NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Camera or photo access
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Social media accounts or login credentials
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking
  • Device identifiers for advertising purposes
  • Any data that can personally identify you

1.2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your device and never leaves your device:

  • Game Progress: Your mastery levels, accuracy statistics, achievements unlocked, and practice session history
  • Settings: Your preferences for game rules, animation speed, sound settings, and selected theme
  • Purchase Status: Whether you have purchased the premium version (stored locally after verification with Google Play or App Store)

This local storage uses your device's standard storage mechanisms (SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS) and cannot be accessed by us or any third party.

2. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect any personal information, we cannot use it for any purpose. All data stored locally on your device is used solely to:

  • Save your game progress and statistics
  • Remember your app settings and preferences
  • Track your achievements and mastery levels
  • Verify your premium purchase status

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 In-App Purchases

When you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by:

  • Google Play Billing (Android devices)
  • Apple App Store (iOS devices)

These services handle all payment information. We never see or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing information. Please refer to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

After a successful purchase, we only store a local flag on your device indicating that the premium version has been unlocked.

3.2 No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising

We do not use any third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.) or advertising networks. Your usage of the app is completely private.

4. Data Security

Since all your data is stored locally on your device and we do not transmit any personal information over the internet, your data security depends on your device's security measures:

  • Keep your device password/PIN protected
  • Keep your operating system updated
  • Download apps only from official stores (Google Play, App Store)

We do not have access to your device or the data stored on it.

5. Children's Privacy

Alis Blackjack is a strategy training simulator with simulated gambling content. While the app does not involve real money gambling, it is rated for users aged 12+ due to the simulated gambling theme.

We do not knowingly collect any information from children or any users of any age. The app functions entirely offline (except for in-app purchases) and does not require registration or personal information.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Data Control

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • Delete All Data: Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data
  • Clear App Data: Use your device's app settings to clear app data without uninstalling
  • Restore Purchases: Use the "Restore Purchases" button in the app to restore your premium status on a new device

6.2 No Data Portability

Because your data never leaves your device and we don't maintain any servers or databases, there is no data to export or port to other services.

7. No Online Features

Alis Blackjack is designed to work completely offline. The only online functionality is:

  • In-app purchase verification (when making a purchase or restoring purchases)

No gameplay data, statistics, or personal information is ever transmitted online.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

If we make material changes that affect how we handle your information, we will notify you through the app or by updating the app description in the store.

The app is purely educational and for entertainment purposes.
