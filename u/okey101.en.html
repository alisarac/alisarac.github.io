Müdavim Okey

Okey 101 against talking regulars or your friends — offline too.

Live

Three regulars who know you.

What it does

A real table

Green felt, an oak istaka, and three regulars who know you — they talk, they tease, they cheer when you open. A cast of twenty-two faces, three new ones at the table each game. On your first hands the one on your left tells you what to do next, once.

Four games at one table

101: open with 101 or five pairs, lay your melds, add to anyone's. Partners 101: the player across is on your side, one of you goes out and both are saved. Classic Okey: fourteen tiles, nothing laid down; when all are melds, throw the last and go out. Partners Okey: classic, played as a team.

With friends

Open a table and send its code or link; your friends sit down with one tap and the regulars take the empty chairs. On the same Wi-Fi you do not even need the internet: the table runs on your phone and the others see it among the tables near them. If your connection drops, a regular plays your turn; come back and carry on with your own hand.

Your table, your rules

Every coffee house plays 101 its own way, so every rule is a switch: rising openings, the tile from the left must go down, doubling for going out with the okey, from pairs or in one go, penalties for throwing the okey or a tile that fits the table, five to eleven hands a game.

A table that helps

Group tiles on your istaka with a gap and a valid meld is underlined with its value; the button shows what you would open with. One Sort button arranges by runs or by pairs. Double-tap a tile to throw it. The tile you drew is marked, the melds your tile fits light up, and a long press on a corner shows what that player has thrown. When a hand ends, one card says who went out, how, and what everyone writes — and why.

Honest money

Free — every game, rule and opponent. An advert only between hands, never during play, no banners, no rewarded video. No adverts at an online table, ever. One purchase, the Regulars Card, removes the adverts and adds marble and porcelain tiles and navy and burgundy cloths. Nothing sold changes a hand.
