Fewer

Pick up your phone less. See your intention, know your count. Minimal launcher.

Live

Pickups, not screen time.

Maker's note

A launcher I wrote for myself. I had been using a minimal launcher for a while; it calmed the phone down, but it was not quite what I wanted. Stripping things away was not enough — I wanted something that pushed.

The difference is this: minimal launchers take things away — icons, colour, notification dots. After taking those away, Fewer puts two things back: what you mean to do today, and how many times you have picked up the phone. Both sit on the home screen. Seeing the number is a brake in itself, nobody wants it to be 90; the sentence reminds you, every time you unlock, that the day is for something.

Asking instead of locking came from the same place. A lock makes you find a way around the lock; a question leaves the answer with you. A screen that asks “how much today, how many minutes will you stay” before a time sink opens, and shows you what you said against what happened when you come back. The rest is yours.

What it does

A deliberately quiet home screen

Three things on the home screen: what you mean to do today, how many times you have picked up the phone so far, and the six tools you actually use. No widgets, no notification dots, no wallpaper, no icons.

The count

Fewer counts how many times a day you pick up the phone, and how many of those were for nothing: unlocked, looked, locked again. The number stays on the home screen. Nobody wants it to be 90.

The sentence

One sentence at the top: what today is for. Finish it and it drops below; the next one comes. At the start of the week Fewer asks a few questions and has you pick five things; each morning's sentence comes from that list.

Time sinks

Tag Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and the like as time sinks. Opening one from the home screen shows a short pause: how much today, how many minutes you'll stay. It doesn't lock; it asks. When you come back you see what you said against what happened.

Day blocks

Morning, work, evening, night. Time sinks meet different friction in each block; at night they can disappear entirely. Blocks follow the clock.

Your data stays on the phone

No server, no account, no ads, no analytics. The usage access permission feeds only the counter and the ranking, on the device; decline it and the app still works.
