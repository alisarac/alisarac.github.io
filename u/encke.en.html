Encke

Thread the debris belts of Saturn's rings by turning the bezel.

Live

The Encke Gap is a real division in Saturn's A ring.

Maker's note

After finishing Kiln I wanted to know what else the same input could do. The bezel gives you exactly one thing: an angle. Reduced to its purest form, that means a game about finding the right angle.

The Encke Gap is not an invented name — it is a real division in Saturn's A ring. The whole game rests on that single idea: each debris belt has one opening, find it and pass through. There is no second mechanic and no menu.

Sticking to one mechanic is a hard decision, but the right one on a watch. You look at your wrist for twenty seconds, and a second rule to learn does not fit in twenty seconds.

What it does

One mechanic, one opening

That gap is this game's only mechanic: find the single opening in each debris belt and pass through it. Turn the bezel to position your ship around the ring and slip through. Belts speed up, gaps narrow.

  • Three lives, and one back every 50 clean passes, up to three
  • Endless run, difficulty tied to distance travelled
  • Eight colour themes
  • No touchscreen needed, the game is played entirely with the bezel

Requirements

Requires a Wear OS watch with a rotating bezel or a rotating crown. The game is played entirely with that input and will not work on touch-only watches.
